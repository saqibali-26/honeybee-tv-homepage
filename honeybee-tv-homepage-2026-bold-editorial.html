02 / Who we are

Distinctivetelevisionwith purpose.

Independent thinking. Open doors.

Honey Bee is an independent television production company making unscripted popular factual programmes for UK and international audiences. We bring together specialist knowledge, unusual access and strong characters to tell stories that connect with people.

From ambitious investigations to surprising everyday worlds, we believe the best television starts with a question worth asking — and the determination to follow it.

03 / People · Planet · Stories

For a brighter tomorrow.

Our name comes from the honey bees living in our hives. Environmental responsibility is part of our identity, and we support work that helps pollinators, beekeeping communities and educational opportunities for children.
